# ADR Identification Basics

## Objective
The objective of ADR identification is to systematically identify and assess suspected adverse drug reactions by reviewing the patient's symptoms, medication history, suspected drug, exposure timeline, and other possible causes.

## Key Information Required

1. Patient information and relevant medical history.
2. Complete medication history, including prescribed and OTC medicines.
3. Suspected drug name, dose, route, and duration of treatment.
4. Date/time of drug exposure and onset of symptoms.
5. Description of the suspected adverse reaction.
6. Concomitant medications and possible drug interactions.
7. Alternative causes that could explain the symptoms.
8. Outcome after stopping or continuing the suspected medicine.
9. Relevant laboratory investigations, if available.

## ADR Identification Process

- Identify the signs and symptoms.
- Check the complete medication history.
- Identify the suspected medicine.
- Establish the drug exposure and symptom-onset timeline.
- Consider alternative causes.
- Assess the relationship between the drug and the reaction.
- Document the suspected ADR appropriately.
